AI Reaction Studio 0.2 development preview

Application and FilmCraft editing engine:
  AI Reaction Studio contributors; ArtCraft Team and FilmCraft contributors
  MIT OR Apache-2.0. Full texts are included as LICENSE-MIT and LICENSE-APACHE.
  https://github.com/stuartraymor/AI-Reaction-Studio
  https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft
The editor/ folder contains the unmodified FilmCraft editor from the verified
baseline build, with its original identity. The separate reaction workspace has
its own original icon and branding. FilmCraft itself is alpha software.

Local model runtime (separate process, not a second editing engine):
  llama.cpp b11744, ggml-org contributors, MIT.
  https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/b11744
  Full runtime notices are in runtime/LICENSE-llama.cpp and
  runtime/LICENSE-LLVM-OpenMP (Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions).

Models are NOT bundled. The app's Download local models action downloads the
pinned files shown in models.json and FilmCraft's upstream model catalogs:
  OpenAI Whisper tiny: MIT; official HF conversion Apache-2.0.
  HuggingFaceTB SmolVLM2-500M and ggml-org GGUF conversion: Apache-2.0.
  Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Unsloth GGUF conversion): Apache-2.0.
  hexgrad Kokoro-82M weights/voices: Apache-2.0 as declared on model card.
  CMUdict: Carnegie Mellon University BSD-style license.
No model output is guaranteed accurate. Transcripts, visual observations and
commentary must be reviewed. No independent source verification is implemented.

NSIS builds this installer. NSIS is distributed under its zlib/libpng license;
the NSIS compiler is a build dependency and is not installed by this package.

Rust dependencies retain their upstream permissive licenses. See Cargo.lock,
the repository's dependency declarations and upstream source notices.
No FFmpeg binary is shipped or used by the application's production pipeline.
Windows and the Microsoft VC++ runtime are platform prerequisites; this installer
checks for vcruntime140.dll and does not download or install system components.

This preview installer is unsigned. Projects, settings and downloaded models are
retained on uninstall in the user's AI Reaction Studio data/project folders.
